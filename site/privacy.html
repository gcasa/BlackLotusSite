Privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026

About this policy

This policy describes how Black Lotus Incorporated handles information on this website and in communications you send to us. The Clock & Ledger and ScoreComposer app policies are included below. Our other downloadable apps and separately hosted services, including SalonSync, may have different data practices. Refer to the privacy information provided with each product or service for those details.

Clock & Ledger app privacy

Clock & Ledger stores your business details, clients, time entries, invoices, expenses, receipt attachments, and preferences locally on your Mac. The app does not operate a developer-hosted account or cloud service and does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not receive your ledger through normal use of the app.

When you choose to prepare or send an invoice or enable reminders, the app uses Apple Mail to pass invoice information and PDF attachments to the recipients you configure. Your Mail provider and the recipient may receive that information. Mail automation requires your permission. The app checks enabled Mail account addresses to validate the sender; it does not read your inbox messages.

Files you import are processed locally. Backups, exported reports, PDFs, and receipt exports are saved where you choose. You control those copies and any sharing or backup services used for their storage. Ledger files and exports may contain personal and financial information and are not encrypted by the app; protect them using your Mac's security and storage settings.

You can edit or delete records using the app's controls, subject to the billing-history safeguards described in its documentation. Removing the app does not necessarily remove its ledger, backups, or exported files. Keep a backup before manually deleting app data.

Apple handles App Store purchases and may process purchase or diagnostic information under its own privacy policies. This policy describes the app's own data practices.

For privacy questions, contact Gregory Casamento at greg.casamento@gmail.com. If you contact support, include only the information needed to explain the issue; ledger files are not required for general support.

ScoreComposer app privacy

ScoreComposer processes scores, imported MIDI and MusicXML files, audio rendering, patches, and preferences locally on your Mac. It requires no account and includes no developer-operated cloud service, analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs. The developer does not receive your compositions or MIDI performances through normal use.

You choose files to open, save, print, or export. MIDI devices and optional installed Audio Unit plug-ins process the music you route to them. Third-party plug-ins have their own privacy practices. You control any sharing, cloud storage, or backup services used with your files.

Projects, exported files, and preferences remain until you remove them using the app, Finder, or macOS settings. Removing the app may leave those files in place. Apple handles App Store purchases and diagnostics under its own policies. If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send; scores are not required for general support.

Information you share with us

You can browse this website without creating an account or submitting personal information. If you contact us by email, we receive your email address, the contents of your message, and any information or attachments you choose to send, such as your name, app version, or device details.

We use that information to respond to inquiries, provide support, and discuss or deliver requested services. Please do not send passwords, payment details, or other sensitive information.

Website hosting and technical information

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes. Its handling of that information is described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not set cookies or use analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or browser storage. Images and fonts are served as local website assets.

Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising. Hosting and email providers process information as needed to operate their services. We may disclose information when required by law or as necessary to protect rights and security.

We retain correspondence as needed to handle your request, maintain relevant business records, and meet legal obligations. Retention by hosting and email providers is subject to their own policies.

Links to other services

Links to GitHub, SalonSync, and other external destinations take you to services with their own privacy practices. Review their policies before providing personal information.

Your choices and privacy requests

You choose what to include when you contact us. To ask about personal information you have shared, or request access, correction, or deletion, email greg.casamento@gmail.com. We will consider requests under applicable law and may need to verify your identity. Some records may need to be retained for legal obligations.

Changes and contact

We will post updates to this policy on this page and revise the effective date above. For privacy questions, contact Black Lotus Incorporated at greg.casamento@gmail.com.
